# Icon source

`icon-source.html` is the SVG the PWA icons are rendered from. Open it in a
browser and screenshot the two `svg` elements at 512x512:

- `#std`  -> `public/icon-512.png`, downscaled to `icon-192.png` (192) and
  `apple-touch-icon.png` (180)
- `#mask` -> `public/icon-512-maskable.png` (art pulled into the centred safe
  zone so platform masks cannot clip it)

The design: four bubbles ascending left to right, growing as they rise and
warming from ember through amber to dawn gold on the app indigo. It keeps the
bubble language of the ChoreBubbles family while the warm ascending sweep is
unmistakable beside ChoreBubbles cool centred clump. The rise is the product:
pressure builds, you act, rhythm climbs.
